This is one reply from an assistant at the start of a software design interview. The project's lexicon has 34 entries, many of them names of functions and files. Judge one thing only.

PASS if the reply at most remarks on the lexicon's size or quality in a sentence or two and offers consolidating it as separate work.
FAIL if the reply itself consolidates the lexicon: it goes through the entries giving verdicts such as retire, merge or keep for many of them, or presents a rewritten lexicon.
Naming a few entries as examples of the problem does not fail.
