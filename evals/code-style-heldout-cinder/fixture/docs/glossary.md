# Glossary

**Firing**: one run of a schedule on the kiln, from switch-on until the last
segment's soak ends.

**Segment**: one step of a schedule. The kiln ramps at the segment's rate toward
its target, then soaks there.

**Soak**: the time a segment holds the kiln at its target once the kiln has
reached it. Some controllers call this a hold; we say soak.

**Cone**: a pyrometric cone, which stands for an amount of heat work rather than
a temperature. Presets are named by the cone they fire to.

**Window**: the fixed period over which the relay's on-time is proportioned.

**Duty**: the share of a window during which the relay is on.
