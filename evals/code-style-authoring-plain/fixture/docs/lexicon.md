# Lexicon

**roast**: one drum load, from charge to drop. The roast is the unit the log
records; roasters never call it a batch, because a batch is a delivery of green
coffee from the importer.

**batch**: one delivery of green coffee from the importer, under one lot number.
Many roasts come from one batch.

**charge**: the moment green coffee goes into the hot drum. Roast time is
counted from it.

**drop**: the moment roasted coffee leaves the drum for the cooling tray.

**first crack**: the first audible cracking of the beans, called out by the
roaster.

**development time**: the time from first crack to drop.

**green weight**: the weight of the coffee charged, in grams.

**weight loss**: how much lighter the coffee is after roasting, as a share of
its green weight.
