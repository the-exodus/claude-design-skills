# Lexicon

**locker bank**: one physical cabinet of compartments at one site, with its own
lock board and scanner. Usually just *bank*.

**compartment**: one lockable door of a bank, in one of three sizes. Never
*slot* or *box*; couriers use those words for other things.

**drop-off**: a courier putting a parcel into a compartment and closing the
door.

**collection code**: the code printed on the customer's receipt that opens the
compartment once.

**dwell time**: how long a parcel has been in its compartment, in whole days,
counted from the door locking at drop-off.

**return to sender**: what happens to a parcel whose dwell time reaches the
limit: the compartment is marked for the next courier round to empty.
