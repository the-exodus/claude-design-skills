On *reading*: the lexicon is right and I misspoke. gaugefeed puts the fetch time into each reading it prints, and the dashboard uses that; it stamps nothing of its own. *reading* stands as defined.

Yes, the sticky state with two edges is what I meant: a station becomes silent after an hour with no usable reading, and stops being silent after three usable readings in a row, and in between it stays as it is.

In a row of asks. To keep recovery quick, a silent station that answers once goes back to being asked every cycle, until it either makes three in a row or misses again.

The station that answers every 59 minutes: fine to ignore in this version. Record it as deferred.
