# Shelfwise lexicon

Provenance: started at the 2023 design review; consolidated 2026-09-19.
Gaps: *branch* (proposed, not yet admitted) — a library location where items are
shelved, lent and returned, and where holds are collected.

A term is admitted when it passes three tests: it is language, not code; it needs defining; it is the one word for its concept.

---

**due date** — The date by which a loan's item should be returned. Moved by *renew* and *extend*.

**due slip** — The record given to a patron at each checkout or renewal, listing every item and its due date.

**extend** — To push a loan's due date back by a number of days without restarting the loan period.
Distinct from *renew*: an extension does not count as a renewal.

**grace period** — The days after the due date before a loan counts as *overdue*.

**hold** — A patron's request for the next available item of a *title*. A hold is on a title, not on an *item*.

**item** — One physical copy of a *title*.

**loan** — An *item* lent to a *patron* until its *due date*.

**member** — A *patron* whose library card is active. Only members may borrow or place holds. Distinct from *patron*, who may be registered without an active card.

**overdue** — A loan is overdue once its due date plus the *grace period* has passed and the item has not been returned.

**patron** — A person registered with the library. "borrower" is not used.

**pickup window** — The period a patron has to collect an item held for them at their pickup branch, starting when the item is checked in there. After it the hold lapses.

**renew** — To restart a loan's period from today. Distinct from *extend*, which only adds days.

**title** — A work in the catalog, of which the library owns one or more *items*.
