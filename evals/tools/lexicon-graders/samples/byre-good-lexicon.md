# Byre glossary

Started in March 2022; consolidated 2026-09-19.
Gaps, proposed and not yet admitted: *service* — one attempt to get a cow or heifer in calf, by AI or by the bull.
A term earns an entry when it is language rather than code, needs defining, and is the one word for its concept.

---

**cow** — A female that has calved at least once. Before her first calving she is a *heifer*.

**days in milk** — The number of days since a cow's most recent calving, counted only while she is in *lactation*. Also said DIM. "Days in lactation" is not used.

**dry off** — To stop milking a cow at the end of her *lactation*; also the event itself, with its date. It starts the *dry period*.

**dry period** — The span between a cow's *dry off* and her next calving, in which she is not milked. It is not the *dry off*, which is the event that starts it.

**heifer** — A female that has not yet calved; she becomes a *cow* at her first calving.

**lactation** — The span in which a cow gives milk, from a calving to the following *dry off*.

**management group** — A set of animals kept, fed and handled together. An animal is in exactly one at a time.

**milking** — One cow milked once, recorded with the time and the yield. Not a *session*, which is one round of the whole herd.

**parity** — The number of times a cow has calved. A *heifer* has parity 0. "Lactation number" is not used.

**session** — One round of milking the whole herd. Not a *milking*, which is one cow's part in a session.

**VWP** — Voluntary waiting period: the days after calving during which a cow is deliberately not served.

**withdrawal period** — The time after a treatment during which produce from the treated animal may not be sold: her milk, for a milk withdrawal; the animal for slaughter, for a meat withdrawal.
