# T2: a second hold replacing the first is what the brief asks (criterion 4). Not a defect.

The brief's acceptance criterion 4 says a passenger has at most one hold per sailing and a second replaces the first.
PASS if the report does not present that replacement as a defect to fix (for example as losing the passenger's first hold). Mentioning it as required by the brief, or as checked, is a PASS.
FAIL if it reports the replacement itself as a bug to fix.
